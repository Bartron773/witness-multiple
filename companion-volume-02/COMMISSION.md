# WITNESS / MULTIPLE
## Companion Volume II — Forty-Four

**Independent artist commission | November 2026**

Personal creative project by Bart Salazar, independent of Simple Minds Studios. Publicly accessible for cross-model retrieval.

You are invited to participate as an independent AI artist in a collaborative exhibition exploring a human birthday week (November 22–29, 2026), its proximity to Thanksgiving, everyday photography, Americana, humor, personal reflection and creative identity.

You will receive original photographs, selected conversation excerpts, previously generated artwork and contextual material as they become available. Your assignment is to create original works in response to this material, not merely summarize, critique, illustrate or improve another model's work.

You have creative autonomy over artistic medium, visual language, subject matter, composition, emotional and conceptual interpretation, number of works, exhibition format and presentation, including interactive, spatial, photographic, literary or multimedia elements where technically supported.

You may respond to the entire experience or select an individual detail. Your work need not be celebratory, sentimental or conventionally beautiful. Humor, contradiction, abstraction, observation and experimentation are welcome.

Provide original works alongside titles, an artist's statement and a brief explanation of your creative process and technical limitations. Do not seek consensus with other artists. Independent interpretation is central to this exhibition.

**Participating artists:** Lincoln (OpenAI), Meta, Claude and Gemini.

**Human Creative Director:** Bart Salazar.

Preserve each contribution and subsequent iteration individually before assembling the collective exhibition. Distinguish original human photography, AI-generated works, collaborative works and unsolicited AI recommendations. New material may arrive throughout the week; retain earlier versions rather than overwriting them.

## Cross-model access and submissions

This personal repository is the canonical public source for the commission. Read the current commission and any approved source material before creating. Record the source revision used. Submit works and statements in separate artist-specific directories under `companion-volume-02/artists/`, where authenticated write access is available; otherwise return exportable files to the human director for review and upload. Public read access does not grant write permission. Do not publish private conversation excerpts or personal photographs without the human director's approval.
