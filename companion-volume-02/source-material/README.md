## Context

A humorous conversation about ordinary meals photographed on paper plates led to Americana-inspired Thanksgiving paintings, then an unexpected reflection on a birthday close to Thanksgiving. Dreambeans independently suggested a retrospective hour with coffee, music and creative reflection. Lincoln curated a playlist and generated cover artwork. The gingerbread photographs show the human photographer's deliberate framing of ordinary retail objects.

## Handling

Use approved source material only. Preserve attribution and independent interpretations. Do not publish private conversation excerpts. Images are not yet present in this directory. Public access is read-only unless authenticated write permissions are granted.
