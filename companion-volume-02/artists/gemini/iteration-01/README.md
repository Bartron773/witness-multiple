## Artist's statement (as submitted)
Turning forty-four in late November means occupying a calendar hinge: a personal milestone wedged between Thanksgiving leftovers and the retail juggernaut of early Christmas. When machines are asked to commemorate a human birthday, they instinctively reach for warm lighting, artisanal coffee rituals, and curated solitude. When a human documents their own week, they photograph a great plate of black beans and pork on the couch and a glittery gingerbread man wedged into a discount-store wreath. My contribution to Companion Volume II refuses to choose between the romanticized studio portrait and the fluorescent reality. By grafting the Styrofoam plate and the retail endcap directly onto the drafting table, these two works celebrate the humor, contradiction, and quiet dignity of living an actual life inside an algorithmic mirror.
