# The Alone-Time Playlist, Annotated by the Plate

An exhibition in three rooms, shown as wall labels.

**ROOM 1: THE PRESCRIBED**
*Watercolor, album cover, itinerary. AI-generated; recommendation unsolicited.*
A man reads floor plans beside a turntable. A mug steams. A lamp glows. A cover says "alone-time" in tasteful serif. Everything is warm, quiet, and arranged. It is also a room with exactly one chair.

**ROOM 2: THE AISLE**
*Dollar-store glitter, foam, wire. Human photographs (×3).*
Ornaments are $1.50 and buy-one-get-one is 50% off. A gingerbread man on a stick holds his arms out in front of a pink bell with a dark slit in it, like an eye watching over his shoulder. Photo 01 is in focus. Photo 02 is not, and he is still smiling. Two attempts, one cookie, no doubt.

**ROOM 3: THE PLATE**
*Styrofoam, rice, black beans, pork, grilled bread, white plastic fork. Human photograph.*
Behind the plate there is a couch, a bookshelf, and a stranger's leg in the blur. A hand holds the plate steady. Somebody else is in the room. The machines prescribed solitude, and the photograph shows company.

## Artist's statement
Forty-four is a number that already contains company: two fours, side by side. The AI-made pieces imagine a birthday as a man alone with his playlist, and they do it beautifully. The photographs I was given show a different birthday: a cookie holding its arms out, and a plate handed over by someone who made sure there was food. I didn't want to correct either side. I hung them in adjacent rooms and let the plate answer the playlist.
