# The Extraordinary Ordinary
Three original minimalist vector studies: The Offering, Manufactured Joy, The Occasion. Attention is an act of valuation. The extraordinary does not always require transformation; sometimes it requires everything surrounding it to be removed. The empty plate is a deliberate abstraction, not a reproduction of the third-party photograph. The ornament and chair are symbolic reductions. SVG authored in chat, not photographic work.
