## Purpose

The independent artifacts were produced without the witnesses reading one another's Commission 06 contributions.

The purpose of this phase is to preserve what becomes visible only after those independent accounts are placed beside one another.

This is not a consensus exercise.

Agreement should not be treated as truth.
Difference should not be treated as error.
Similarity should not be assumed to mean identical reasoning.

The Comparative Field exists to examine relationships among independently produced arguments while preserving their differences.

Your task is not to determine which witness was correct.
